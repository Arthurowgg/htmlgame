How to Play

Controls

  • WASD — move
  • Mouse — look (click to lock)
  • Space — jump
  • F / Left click — attack
  • E — talk / gather
  • M — map · Q — quests
  • Esc — pause
  • Shift — sprint

Goal

Explore the island, complete the story quests, collect ten artifacts, and defeat three bosses to restore the Crown of Light.

Tips

  • Talk to Elder Mira first.
  • Flowers grow in Sun Meadow — gather them for quests.
  • Hearts drop from foes; gold buys nothing yet but unlocks challenges.
  • Night falls slowly — shadows hit harder in the dark.
